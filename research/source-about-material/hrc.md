## Wikipedia summary
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Campaign  ·  Accessed: 2026-05-30  ·  Confidence: VERIFIED

The Human Rights Campaign (HRC) is an American LGBTQ advocacy group. It is the largest LGBTQ political lobbying organization within the United States. Based in Washington, D.C., the organization focuses on protecting and expanding rights for LGBTQ individuals, including advocating for same-sex marriage, anti-discrimination and hate crimes legislation, and HIV/AIDS advocacy. It also considers abortion access a "fundamental human right" and a core component of LGBTQ+ healthcare. The organization has a number of legislative initiatives as well as supporting resources for LGBTQ individuals.
