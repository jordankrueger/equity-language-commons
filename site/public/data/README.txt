elc-index.sqlite is a build-time export of the Equity Language Commons.

The cross-reference layer (term pages, syntheses, paraphrases) is CC BY 4.0, Jordan Krueger.
Source guides remain (c) their respective publishers. Quotes are used under fair use,
each capped at 50 words, with attribution in the org_slug, year and quote_loc columns.
Reuse of quoted material beyond citation requires the publisher's permission.

The same notice is stored in the database's meta table.
Methodology: https://equitylanguagecommons.org/methodology/
