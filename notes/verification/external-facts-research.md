## AP Stylebook family

- **A1 — AP dropped "illegal immigrant" (April 2, 2013): VERIFIED.**
  Source: Poynter, https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2013/ap-changes-style-on-illegal-immigrant/
  Excerpt: "use *illegal* only to refer to an action, not a person: *illegal*
  immigration, but not *illegal immigrant*." Also banned "illegal alien,"
  "an illegal," "illegals" outside direct quotes. No stable ap.org URL for
  the original Kathleen Carroll announcement; Poynter is the canonical record.
- **A2 — singular they: 2017 VERIFIED; "2019 expansion" UNVERIFIED.**
  Source: Poynter, https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2017/ap-style-change-singular-they-is-acceptable-in-limited-cases/
  Excerpt: "They/them/their is acceptable in limited cases as a singular
  and/or gender-neutral pronoun, when alternative wording is overly awkward
  or clumsy." **No source found for a distinct 2019 expansion — drop the
  2019 claim from the pronouns page.**
- **A3 — AP dropped dual-heritage hyphen (April 2019): VERIFIED.**
  Source: AP Stylebook on X, https://x.com/APStylebook/status/1121055621980336128
  Excerpt: "We no longer use a hyphen in dual heritage terms including
  African American and Asian American."
- **A4 — capital-B Black (June 19, 2020), lowercase white (July 2020), NABJ
  recommending both: VERIFIED.**
  Sources: https://www.niemanlab.org/2020/06/ap-style-is-now-to-capitalize-black-and-indigenous/ ;
  https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2020/the-associated-press-announced-it-will-not-capitalize-w-in-white/ ;
  https://nabjonline.org/blog/nabj-statement-on-capitalizing-black-and-other-racial-identifiers/
  Note the two-part timeline: Black announced June 19 (Juneteenth); the
  white decision separately in July.

## Pew Research family

- **P1 — 2013 survey (50% no preference; Hispanic over Latino ~2:1 [33% vs
  15% of all respondents]; Texas 46% vs 8%): VERIFIED.**
  Source: https://www.pewresearch.org/race-and-ethnicity/2013/10/22/3-hispanic-identity/
  Survey May 24–July 28, 2013, n=5,103. Precision: the 2:1 ratio is among
  all respondents (33%/15%); Texas ratio is ~6:1 (46/8) with 44% no pref.
- **P2 — 2019 survey, published Aug 11, 2020 (3% use Latinx; 23% heard of
  it): VERIFIED from the primary PDF.**
  Source: https://www.pewresearch.org/hispanic/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2020/08/PHGMD_2020.08.11_Latinx_FINAL.pdf
  Excerpt: "only 23% of U.S. adults who self-identify as Hispanic or Latino
  have heard of the term Latinx, and just 3% say they use it to describe
  themselves." Survey Dec 3–23, 2019, n=3,030.
- **P3 — age gradient: VERIFIED.** Same PDF p.7: 18–29 awareness 42% vs 7%
  for 65+; usage 7% (18–29) vs <0.5% (65+).
- **P4 — preference stability: VERIFIED with framing caveat.**
  Same PDF p.8: "In more than 15 years of polling … half … have consistently
  said they have no preference." 2024 follow-up (different question framing):
  https://www.pewresearch.org/race-and-ethnicity/2024/09/12/u-s-hispanics-preferences-among-pan-ethnic-terms/

## Statutes / legal

- **L1 — H.R. 4238 / PL 114-157 (signed May 20, 2016): VERIFIED with scope
  precision.** Source: https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/house-bill/4238
  It modernized terms in exactly TWO 1970s acts (DOE Organization Act;
  Local Public Works Act 1976) — e.g. "American Indian"→"Native American",
  "Oriental"→"Asian American", "Negro"→"African American". It did NOT sweep
  federal Indian law (18 USC 1151, ISDA etc. unchanged). Do not overclaim.
- **L2 — 18 U.S.C. § 1151 "Indian country": VERIFIED.**
  Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1151 (three-part
  jurisdictional definition; statute lowercases "country").
- **L3 — McKinney-Vento "fixed, regular, and adequate nighttime residence":
  VERIFIED.** General definition at 42 U.S.C. § 11302 (education-specific at
  § 11434a(2)). Source: https://nche.ed.gov/mckinney-vento-definition/
- **L4 — Canada's Indian Act in force, "Indian" as statutory category:
  VERIFIED.** Source: https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-5/FullText.html
  ("Indian means a person who pursuant to this Act is registered as an
  Indian or is entitled to be registered as an Indian.")
- **L5 — USCIS 2021 move to "noncitizen": VERIFIED for 2021, REVERSED in
  2025.** Policy Manual technical update May 11, 2021 replaced "alien" with
  "noncitizen" where possible; the 2025 administration reinstated "alien."
  **Frame as the 2021 Biden-era update, not current practice.**
- **L6 — DREAM Act 2001 / never enacted; DACA 2012 (DHS Napolitano memo, not
  an executive order): VERIFIED. Recipient counts: PARTIAL — UPDATE NUMBERS.**
  Active DACA recipients ~538,000 (KFF, as of Sept 30, 2024:
  https://www.kff.org/racial-equity-and-health-policy/key-facts-on-deferred-action-for-childhood-arrivals-daca/ );
  the 580–600k figure is stale (2017–18 peak). Total Dreamer population ~2.6M
  childhood arrivals (FWD.us: https://www.fwd.us/news/dreamers-by-the-numbers/ );
  "2M+" conflates DACA-eligible vs DREAM-Act-eligible pools — specify.

## Historical events

- **H1 — "institutional racism" coined by Carmichael & Hamilton, *Black
  Power* (1967): VERIFIED.** (Full title: *Black Power: The Politics of
  Liberation in America*.)
- **H2 — Two-Spirit, Winnipeg 1990: VERIFIED with nuance.** Emerged from a
  series of five conferences culminating at the Third Annual Inter-tribal
  Native American, First Nations, Gay and Lesbian American Conference,
  Winnipeg 1990. **Say "adopted at," not "coined at."** Individual
  attribution disputed (Albert McLeod / Myra Laramee) — do not attribute.
- **H3 — Blumenbach 1795 third edition, five-race typology, cranial basis,
  Caucasus-origin theory: VERIFIED.** (1775 first edition had four.)
- **H4 — NAACP founded 1909, retains "Colored People": VERIFIED.**
  Source: https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/naacp/founding-and-early-years.html
- **H5 — Republic Records dropped "urban" June 5, 2020: VERIFIED. "Other
  industry bodies followed": PARTIAL — Billboard/Apple/Spotify had already
  moved BEFORE Republic.** Source: https://variety.com/2020/music/news/republic-records-urban-term-black-music-1234626400/
  Reframe any cascade implication.
- **H6 — UNITY 1994 objection to "illegal alien": UNVERIFIED.** The 1994
  UNITY resolution on record concerns Native sports mascots, not immigration
  terminology. Earliest documented NAHJ campaign ≈2006; SPJ resolution 2017.
  **Remove or re-attribute the 1994 claim on illegal-alien.**
- **H7 — India population: VERIFIED at "over 1.4 billion"** (current
  estimates 1.46–1.48B; don't say "approximately 1.4").
- **H8 — "Federal demographic categories use Caucasian as a formal label":
  CONFIRMED FALSE.** OMB 1997 standards and 2024 revision both use "White":
  https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/omb/fedreg_1997standards
  The softer legacy claim is defensible: FBI UCR used "Caucasian" before its
  2013 overhaul; some HHS-affiliated instruments (Health and Retirement
  Study) used "White/Caucasian"; >1,500 PubMed articles used it in 2023
  (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12270528/).
- **H9 — Rosa's Law (Oct 5, 2010): VERIFIED with scope caveat.** Replaced
  "mental retardation" with "intellectual disability" in statutes under the
  Senate HELP Committee's jurisdiction; full federal coverage followed in
  later steps. Avoid "all federal law."
